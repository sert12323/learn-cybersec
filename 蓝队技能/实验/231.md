### cs规则

![image-20260329173912091](images/image-20260329173912091.png)

msg：提示信息

调用checksunm8_chck.lua  

调用lua检查uri是否符合checksum8规则：计算uri的ascii之和并与256做取余计算，余数为92则符合规则

![image-20260329174122071](images/image-20260329174122071.png)

在config文件中配置 创建\lua_script文件夹放入checksunm8_chck.lua  

![image-20260329182142360](images/image-20260329182142360.png)

修改运行规则为假

![image-20260329175822408](images/image-20260329175822408.png)

```
suricata.exe -c suricata.yaml -s cobaltstrike.rules -i 192.168.182.146
```
